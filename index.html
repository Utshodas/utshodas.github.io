About

I'm a Computer Science graduate (2019-2025) from Shahjalal University of Science and Technology, Bangladesh, with research interests in usable security and privacy, cybersecurity education, and how end-users build mental models of AI systems' behavior and permissions.

My recent work includes CyQured (SOUPS 2026), a tabletop game I designed and empirically evaluated for personal cybersecurity education, and an ongoing project examining how end-users understand AI agent permissions across messaging, productivity, and financial platforms, using interviews, think-aloud studies, and card-sorting tasks.

I currently work as an Associate Database Engineer at Therap(BD) Ltd., where I administer Oracle databases for a healthcare platform, implementing security controls, access restrictions, and compliance measures to protect sensitive patient data.

What draws me to a PhD is a question that goes beyond any one job or project: why systems that are provably secure so often fail the people who have to use them, and how research can close that gap by helping people build accurate, trustworthy models of the systems around them.
